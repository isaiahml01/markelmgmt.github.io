The Story

From South Side Stories To Full-Service Media.

I grew up on the South Side of Chicago, where every block, storefront, and family had a story worth telling—even if nobody was putting a camera on it yet. That lens shaped everything: how I see people, how I see homes, and how I see opportunity in places other people overlook.

I went on to study Television: Executive Production & Entrepreneurship at Columbia College Chicago, learning how to develop shows, pitch ideas, and think like a producer—not just a person with a camera. At the same time, I was falling in love with real estate: the contracts, the negotiations, and the way one closing can literally change someone’s life.

Markel Media & Management lives at that intersection: the structure of real estate, the emotion of film, and the discipline of brand strategy. I don’t just want your content to “look good.” I want it to make sense for the deals you’re chasing, the communities you’re serving, and the version of yourself you’re becoming.

Whether I’m documenting a listing, a live event, or a founder’s story, my goal is the same: make the work feel like you—and make it work for you.

How We Think About The Work.

Markel Media & Management isn’t chasing trends for the sake of it. We care about whether your media moves the right people—buyers, clients, attendees, investors—from curiosity to commitment.

Story Over Aesthetic.

Beautiful visuals matter, but if they don’t communicate who you are and what you do, they become noise. We start with the story, then build the shot list.

Deals & Decisions.

Coming from real estate, I know that a listing video or photo set is part of a larger transaction. We ask, “What decision is this asset supposed to help someone make?”

Systems, Not One-Offs.

Whether it’s a content workshop or a launch campaign, we design media with reuse in mind—clips, cut-downs, and assets that keep serving you after the first post.

Rooted In Chicago.

Chicago is more than a backdrop; it’s part of the story. The way we frame a block, a building, or a room is shaped by the city that built us.
