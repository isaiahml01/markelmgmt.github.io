Logistics, Turnaround & Coverage

What areas do you cover?

We’re based in Chicago and regularly shoot throughout the city, the South Suburbs, and nearby markets like Joliet. For properties outside that radius, travel fees may apply.

How fast can I get my photos and video?

Standard photo-only shoots are delivered within 48 hours. Photo + video packages are typically delivered within 3 business days. Rush delivery is available when you’re up against the clock.

Who owns the content?

You receive a license to use the media for your listing, social platforms, email, and personal marketing. If you’re working with a brokerage or brand that needs broader usage, we’ll make sure the license fits the deal.

Do you help with social media strategy too?

Yes. Beyond capturing the visuals, we can advise on how to roll out your media across Reels, TikTok, YouTube, and email—especially if you’re building a content system for your whole business, not just one listing.
