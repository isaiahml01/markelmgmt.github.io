Workshop Logistics

Where do you host workshops?

Most workshops are hosted on-site at your office, co-working space, or event venue in the Chicago area and South Suburbs. Virtual options are available for teams outside this radius.

What gear do participants need?

At minimum, a smartphone and basic access to the platforms you’re posting on. If you have cameras or studio space, we’ll incorporate them—but we build the system to work even on your busiest day.

Can you tailor it for our niche?

Yes. Before we lock in a date, we’ll talk through your industry, audience, and goals so the examples and prompts feel like your world—not generic social media advice.

Do you offer follow-up support?

We can add optional virtual office hours or a short follow-up series so your team has a place to ask questions once they’ve tried the system on their own.

Ready To Bring Shoot • Post • Close To Your People?

Tell us about your team, your event date, and what “success” looks like—more listings, more leads, better storytelling, or all of the above. We’ll follow up with a proposed format and quote.
