Public Education Workshop

Buying a Home in Illinois — Today

A free, in-person educational workshop designed to replace confusion with clarity — without pressure, hype, or sales tactics.

Reserve a Seat

What This Is (And Isn’t)

This is a calm, structured walk-through of how buying a home in Illinois actually works — costs, timelines, credit, and decision points — explained in sequence, not fragments.

  • • Not a sales seminar
  • • Not a lender pitch
  • • No urgency tactics or pressure

What You’ll Learn

  • • How the Illinois home-buying process actually unfolds
  • • The four costs buyers commonly confuse
  • • How cash-to-close is calculated
  • • How credit and debt-to-income function mechanically
  • • Where deals stall — and why

Format & Materials

This is a two-hour, seated, discussion-friendly session supported by professionally designed materials.

  • • Workshop packet
  • • Decision diagnostic
  • • Glossary card
  • • Complimentary ebook

Venues & Hosting Spaces

This workshop is hosted in partnership with libraries, college campuses, park districts, and civic spaces across Illinois.

Each venue is selected intentionally to preserve a quiet, focused, educational environment — not a sales or networking atmosphere.

  • • Public libraries and community learning spaces
  • • College and university campuses
  • • Park district and civic education rooms

Venue location details are shared after registration.

About the Facilitator

Isaiah Lesley is a Columbia College Chicago alum (BFA, Television, Class of 2022) and a licensed Illinois real estate professional.

His work sits at the intersection of media, negotiation, and housing education — translating complex systems into understandable frameworks so people can make informed decisions without pressure.

Logistics
