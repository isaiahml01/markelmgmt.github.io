Why This Matters

AI and platforms like Zillow can display listings, but only authentic video from your agents builds trust, showcases personality, and converts leads. Training your team in video marketing ensures your brokerage stands out and future-proofs your agents.

  • Boost listings exposure and social reach

Workshop Outline

3–4 hour hands-on training, designed to make video simple and actionable.

Hour 1 – Phone Video Basics

  • Why video wins in real estate
  • Best phone settings for professional-quality video
  • Using natural daylight, angles, and steady shots

Hour 2 – Hands-On at a Listing

  • Live demo: filming exteriors, interiors, and agent intros
  • Agents practice filming with guided coaching
  • Instant playback and feedback

Hour 3 – Quick Editing & Posting

  • Editing on your phone (CapCut, InShot, Canva)
  • Adding captions, music, and branding
  • Posting strategies for Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts

Hour 4 – Multiply Your Content

  • Turn one listing video into 5+ posts
  • Personal branding tips for consistency
  • Q&A and wrap-up

Deliverables: Polished listing video for broker, usable clips for agents, and a PDF cheat sheet with shot lists, posting guide, and workflow tips.
