Real Estate Video Workshop

Hands-on training to empower agents to capture listings and personal brand content with ease.

Learning Outcomes

  • Confidently shoot high-quality video content with their phone.
  • Film listing tours, reels, and client updates that build trust & attract leads.
  • Edit & post quickly using simple, free/low-cost apps.
  • Re-purpose one video across multiple platforms (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, LinkedIn).
  • Develop a consistent on-camera presence that boosts personal brand.

Agenda

Hour 1 – Phone Video Basics

  • Why video sells in real estate.
  • Best phone settings for quality.
  • Using natural daylight, angles, and steady shots.

Hour 2 – Hands-On at a Listing

  • Live demo: capturing exteriors, interiors, and agent intros.
  • Each agent films clips with guided coaching.
  • Instant playback + feedback.

Hour 3 – Quick Editing & Posting

  • Fast editing on phone (CapCut / InShot).
  • Adding captions & music.
  • Posting workflow: IG, TikTok, YouTube Shorts.

Hour 4 – Multiply Your Content

  • Turn one listing video into 5+ posts.
  • Personal branding tips for consistency.
  • Q&A + wrap-up.

Additional Info

  • 3–4 hour, hands-on training session.
  • No need for reserved office space or catering; lunch included.
  • Hosted at one of your current listings so agents walk away with promotional material same-day.
  • Quarterly packages starting at $12,000/year (customizable based on brokerage size).
  • Optional add-ons: editing support, social content templates, recruitment video package, gear kit setup, one-on-one coaching.
