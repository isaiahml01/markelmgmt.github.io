The Show

A Room For The Ones Who Kept Going.

WIWGU was born in the tension between vision and reality—when the calling feels loud but the circumstances feel loud too. It’s a space to say the quiet parts out loud, name the fear, and still choose faith and forward motion.

Each episode moves like a conversation, a prayer, and a strategy session in one. We talk about housing, money, identity, creativity, and calling like they belong in the same sentence—because they do in real life.

Who It’s For

• Builders who feel called to more—business, ministry, media, or all three.
• Creatives trying to honor their art and their bills at the same time.
• People who love God but also love strategy, spreadsheets, and soft life.

If you’ve ever whispered “I can’t keep doing this” and still showed up the next day, you’re in the right place.
